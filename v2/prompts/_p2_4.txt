## Section 7 — Proposal Autopsy (Why a Deal Died)

### P091 — Six-Factor Autopsy Scorecard
```text
Act as a sales manager running a lost-deal review. Here is what happened: [SOURCE]. Deal: [COMPANY], started by [TRIGGER_EVENT], solving [PAIN], priced at [PRICE], proof presented: [PROOF].
Output format (table):
FACTOR | SCORE /5 | EVIDENCE (quote from [SOURCE], max 12 words) | WHAT A 5 LOOKED LIKE (max 10 words)
Factors: discovery, decision-maker access, value framing, proof, price, urgency.
Then: VERDICT — the one factor that lost it (max 12 words).
Hard constraint: no score above 5, no factor may be scored without quoted evidence, no adjectives, output under 450 words.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P092 — Feedback Request Email
```text

SUBJECT (max 6 words) / OPENING — no hard feelings, one line / 5 QUESTIONS — numbered, each answerable in one line, none answerable yes/no / CLOSE — a genuine thank-you without asking to be reconsidered
Hard constraint: 90 words maximum in the body, no adjectives, one question mark per line only, no "we would still love to work with you", and question 1 must ask what would have made the decision easier.

```

### P093 — Loss-Reason Classifier

```text
RULED OUT — 2 reasons with one-line evidence each (max 10 words)

Hard constraint: exactly one primary reason, no reason may be picked without a quote, no adjectives, no exclamation marks, output under 400 words.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P094 — Price vs Value Diagnosis
