## Section 5 — Video Title & Thumbnail Text Pairing

### P061 — Ten Titles, Three Thumbnail Texts
```text
You write titles that survive a skim. For a video on [TOPIC] aimed at [AUDIENCE] on [PLATFORM], produce 10 titles and 3 thumbnail text options.
Output format:
TITLES — numbered list, each max 55 characters, with the keyword placement marked (front/middle)
THUMBNAIL TEXT — 3 options, each max 4 words, no number may repeat the title
PAIRING NOTE — for each thumbnail: which title it pairs with and why (max 10 words)
Constraints: no title may exceed 55 characters, no clickbait words (you won't believe, shocking), no overlap between thumbnail text and title wording.
```

### P062 — Curiosity Gap Without Clickbait
```text
Write 8 titles for [TOPIC] that create a curiosity gap while keeping an honest promise the video actually delivers.
Output format:
TITLE (max 55 chars) | THE GAP (what the viewer does not know yet, max 8 words) | THE PAYOFF (where it is answered: timestamp or section) | TRUTHFUL? (yes/no)
Constraints: every title must be answerable by the video's actual content, no hyperbole, banned: this will change everything, the truth about, exposed, no exclamation marks, total output under 300 words.
```

### P063 — Number-Led Listicle Titles
```text
You optimise listicle titles. Create 7 number-led titles for a [LENGTH] video listing [TOPIC] items for [AUDIENCE].
Rules: the number must be honest to the video, the noun must be specific to [NICHE] (not "things" or "tips"), and each title must read in under 3 seconds.
Output format:
TITLE (max 50 chars) | THE NUMBER | THE SPECIFIC NOUN | SEARCH PHRASE IT TARGETS (max 4 words)
Constraints: no repeated number across titles, no "top" without a qualifier, total under 250 words.
```

### P064 — Honest Niche Titles
```text
Write 8 plain-spoken titles for [PLATFORM] about [TOPIC] that would work on a channel with no personal brand — the promise alone carries the click.
Output format:
TITLE (max 55 chars) | FORMAT (tutorial, comparison, story, list) | WHY A STRANGER CLICKS (max 10 words)
Constraints: no personal names, no "I", no adjectives that cannot be verified, no clickbait markers, at least 4 titles must front-load a keyword a searcher would type.
```

### P065 — Three-Way A/B/C Title Test
```text
You run title tests. For the same video about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE], write 5 variants in each of three styles.
Output format:
A: SEARCH-FIRST — 5 titles, keyword in the first 3 words, max 55 chars each
B: BROWSE-FIRST — 5 titles, curiosity + stakes, max 55 chars each
C: BRANDED — 5 titles, series-like, consistent structure, max 55 chars each
TEST NOTE — which style fits a new channel vs an established one (one line)
Constraints: 15 titles total, no two may share their first 3 words, banned words: best, ultimate, guide, everything you need to know.
```

### P066 — Thumbnail Text and Face Pairing
```text
Act as a thumbnail designer. For a video on [TOPIC] with this footage: [SOURCE], propose 5 thumbnail concepts.
Output format:
CONCEPT 1 — TEXT (max 4 words) / FACE OR SUBJECT (expression + framing, max 10 words) / BACKGROUND (max 8 words) / CONTRAST PLAN (two hex-ish colours in words) / PAIRS WITH TITLE:
(concepts 2-5 same fields)
Constraints: text never repeats the title verbatim, text max 4 words, one focal point per concept, every concept must be legible at 120px wide — state how in the CONTRAST PLAN.
```

### P067 — Title/Thumbnail Conflict Fix
```text
Here is my title [TITLE] and thumbnail text [THUMB]. They repeat each other and waste the click.
Output format:
DIAGNOSIS — what is duplicated (max 12 words)
FIX A — keep the title, change the thumbnail: TEXT (max 4 words) / VISUAL:
FIX B — keep the thumbnail, change the title: (max 55 chars)
FIX C — restructure both: TITLE / THUMB TEXT / HOW THEY COMPLEMENT (max 12 words)
Constraints: no fix may repeat wording between title and thumbnail, no fix may exceed 55 characters in the title, output under 250 words.
```

### P068 — Search-Intent Title
```text
You write titles for search. Given the search phrase people actually type: [SEARCH_TERM] and video content on [TOPIC], write 6 titles that satisfy the intent without sounding engineered.
Output format:
TITLE (max 60 chars) | INTENT (learn / compare / fix / buy) | WHICH WORD MUST STAY EXACTLY AS TYPED | EXPECTED CTR RISK (low/med/high + 4-word reason)
Constraints: the exact search phrase must appear once in at least 4 titles, no title may exceed 60 characters, no year unless [YEAR] is supplied, total under 280 words.
```

### P069 — Seasonal and Deadline Titles
```text
Write 7 titles tied to a real deadline for [TOPIC]: [DEADLINE] (a date, tax window, sale, season, event).
Output format:
TITLE (max 55 chars) | DEADLINE REFERENCE (exact date or period) | WHY IT IS URGENT NOW (max 10 words)
Constraints: no fake urgency — the deadline must be [DEADLINE] or a named annual event, banned: hurry, don't miss out, last chance, no exclamation marks, at least 3 titles must omit the deadline from the title itself and place it in the description.
```

### P070 — Strong-Take Titles With a Hedge
```text
You title opinion videos responsibly. For a strong take on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE], write 8 titles that carry the opinion without asserting an unproven fact.
Output format:
TITLE (max 55 chars) | THE CLAIM | THE HEDGE (the qualifier that keeps it honest, max 6 words) | EVIDENCE NEEDED (cite [SOURCE] or "opinion")
Constraints: no title may state as fact what the video only argues, banned: why X is wrong, X is a lie, no exclamation marks, output under 300 words.
```

### P071 — Tutorial Title for Search and Browse
```text
Write 6 titles for a how-to video on [GOAL] using [TOOL] for [AUDIENCE].
Output format:
VERSION — SEARCH (keyword first, max 60 chars) / BROWSE (promise first, max 55 chars)
Do this 3 times with 3 different angles (task, problem, outcome).
Constraints: no version may contain both the tool name and the outcome if that pushes it over 60 characters — state which you dropped, no "step-by-step" unless the video has numbered steps, total under 250 words.
```

### P072 — Family-Safe Title Set
```text
You title videos for a family/education channel on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].
Write 8 titles with no fear hooks, no shock language, and nothing a school would object to.
Output format:
TITLE (max 55 chars) | WHAT THE KID LEARNS (max 10 words) | WHY A PARENT CLICKS (max 10 words)
Constraints: banned words: scary, crazy, insane, deadly, destroyed, no exclamation marks, no curiosity gap that relies on danger, at least 3 titles must contain a number, output under 300 words.
```

### P073 — Podcast Clip Titles
```text
You title short clips cut from a podcast episode: [SOURCE] (a 2-4 sentence excerpt). Write 8 titles for clips on [TOPIC].
Output format:
CLIP TITLE (max 55 chars) | THE HOOK LINE FROM THE EXCERPT (quote, max 14 words) | WHO THIS REACHES (max 8 words)
Constraints: titles must be attributable to a real statement in [SOURCE], no invented quotes, no drama the excerpt does not support, no exclamation marks, output under 300 words.
```

### P074 — Shorts Titles Under 40 Characters
```text
Write 15 titles for a Short about [TOPIC] that fit in 40 characters including spaces.
Output format:
TITLE | CHARACTER COUNT | THE FIRST 3 WORDS (must carry the hook)
Constraints: 40 characters maximum — count exactly and print the count for each, no abbreviations that read as typos, no full stops, at least 5 must contain a number, no hashtag in the title, output under 250 words.
```

### P075 — Weak Title Rescue
```text
Act as a title editor. Here are 5 weak titles: [TITLES]. Rewrite each into 3 stronger options for a video about [TOPIC] aimed at [AUDIENCE].
Output format (per original):
ORIGINAL | DIAGNOSIS (max 8 words: vague / no stakes / too long / keyword missing / generic)
OPTION 1 — CURIOUS (max 55 chars)
OPTION 2 — SEARCHABLE (max 60 chars)
OPTION 3 — SPECIFIC (max 55 chars, must contain a number or a named thing)
Constraints: 15 rewrites total, none may copy the original's first 3 words, no clickbait markers, no exclamation marks.
```

## Section 6 — B-Roll & Shot-List Generation

### P076 — Shot List for a 30-Second Reel
```text
You are a shot lister. Build the shot list for a 30-second Reel on [TOPIC] from this script: [SOURCE].
Output format (table):
# | TIME | SHOT (framing + subject, max 10 words) | CAMERA MOVE (static/push/pan/handheld) | DURATION (sec) | AUDIO (VO / room / music beat)
Constraints: 8-12 shots, no shot under 1.5 seconds, every shot must be filmable by one person with a phone, last shot must hold the [CTA] text for 2 seconds.
```

### P077 — B-Roll Shopping List
```text
Write a b-roll shopping list for a video on [TOPIC]: 20 clips the editor will need.
Output format (table):
# | CLIP (max 6 words, filmable) | WHY IT IS NEEDED (max 8 words) | WHERE TO GET IT (film / stock / screen record / generate) | SEARCH KEYWORDS (for stock, max 4 words)
Constraints: exactly 20 clips, no two clips may show the same action, at least 6 must be detail/close-up shots, no clip may be "generic footage of people working".
```

### P078 — Overhead Hands-Only Tutorial Shot List
```text
You plan overhead tutorials. Build a shot list for a hands-only demonstration of [TASK] for [AUDIENCE], based on [SOURCE].
Output format:
SETUP SHOT — table of: gear, surface, light direction, camera height (one line each)
ACTION SHOTS — # | BEAT (max 8 words) | FRAMING | WHEN TO CUT IN | TEXT ON SCREEN (max 5 words)
COMMON MISTAKE SHOTS — 2 shots showing what not to do
Constraints: 10-14 shots, no face required, all shots from one camera position unless stated, total output under 500 words.
```

### P079 — Product Hero Shot List
```text
Build a hero-shot list for [PRODUCT] using one window, a phone and [COLOUR] backdrop.
Output format:
SHOT 1 — SETUP (surface / light angle / prop / distance) | PURPOSE (max 8 words)
SHOTS 2-8 — same fields, rotating through: silhouette, detail macro, in-use, scale reference, flat lay, motion pour/drop, packaging
Constraints: 8 shots, each setup must be describable in 15 words or fewer, no studio lighting vocabulary the reader cannot execute at home, note which shots need [PROOF] text overlaid.
```

### P080 — Establishing Sequence With Continuity
```text
You plan opening sequences. Create a 6-shot establishing sequence for [LOCATION] that orients the viewer before the first spoken line.
Output format:
SHOT — TIME | FRAME (max 10 words) | WHAT IT TELLS THE VIEWER (max 8 words) | LIGHT/DIRECTION NOTE | MATCH CUT OPPORTUNITY (one line)
Constraints: 6 shots, chronological light direction must stay consistent, no two shots may show the same framing size, final shot must land where the host starts speaking, output under 350 words.
```

### P081 — Screen-Record Shot List
```text
Plan the screen-recorded portions of a tutorial on [TASK] in [TOOL] for [AUDIENCE].
Output format:
# | SCREEN (which view/menu, max 8 words) | ACTION (click/type, max 6 words) | ZOOM (which region + %) | ON-SCREEN LABEL (max 5 words) | DURATION (sec)

```

### P082 — Transition Map
```text
You design transitions that carry meaning. Build a 10-transition map for a video on [TOPIC] from this script: [SOURCE].
```

