# Cold Outreach & Sales Prompt Vault — 120 Prompts

120 prompts for first touches, follow-ups, LinkedIn, cold calls, proposals, pricing talks, lost-deal autopsies and reviving dead leads. Every prompt carries the same four personalization slots plus one hard constraint, so the output stays short enough that a human will actually answer it.
Built for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini; paste CRM notes or a call transcript wherever a prompt asks for [SOURCE].

## How to use
- Fill [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN] and [PROOF] with checkable detail — a wrong trigger event is worse than no trigger event.
- Keep the hard constraint line when you re-run; it is what keeps replies possible (short, one idea, no adjectives).
- One idea per touch. Never stack two asks in a single email or DM.
- Ask for the smallest next step: 15 minutes, a yes/no, or one number — never "a conversation about exploring synergies".
- Run the sequence prompt first, then sharpen individual touches with the Section 1 or Section 8 prompts.
- Never invent [PROOF]. If you have no number yet, write "[PROOF: no metric yet]" and go find one before sending.
- Read every draft aloud once; if it sounds like a machine wrote it, re-run with "sound like one human writing to another, plainly".

## Fill-in variables
- [COMPANY] — the prospect's company
- [TRIGGER_EVENT] — the observable change: funding, hire, launch, outage, regulation, job post
- [PAIN] — the problem you remove, phrased in their language
- [PROOF] — one real result, metric or credential
- [YOUR_COMPANY] / [OFFER] — what you sell and how it is packaged
- [NAME] / [ROLE] — recipient name and job title
- [PROOF_SOURCE] — where the proof comes from: case study, review, client logo
- [DAY] / [DATE] — scheduling slots for sequences
- [COMPETITOR] — who they use today, if known
- [PRICE] — your number, when it is relevant
- Any other [BRACKET] inside a prompt is self-explanatory; fill it with real detail.

## Section 1 — Cold Email First Touch

### P001 — Problem-First First Touch
```text
You are an SDR who writes first touches that get replies, not opens. Write a cold email to [NAME], [ROLE] at [COMPANY], referencing the [TRIGGER_EVENT] and the [PAIN] it likely created, then offer one way you remove it, backed by [PROOF].
Structure: subject (max 6 words) / first line (no greeting, no "I hope you're well") / problem line / proof line / one-question ask.
Hard constraint: maximum 90 words in the body, no exclamation marks, one question mark in the whole email.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P002 — Trigger-Event First Touch
```text
Write a cold email that opens on the [TRIGGER_EVENT] at [COMPANY] and connects it to the [PAIN] your [OFFER] removes for teams like theirs. Include [PROOF] as a single number with its source in brackets.
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / BODY with paragraphs of one line each / ASK as a standalone final line.
Hard constraint: maximum 120 words, no adjectives about the prospect's company, no exclamation marks.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P003 — No-Adjectives First Touch
```text
Act as an editor who removes everything that sounds like sales. Write a first-touch email to [NAME] at [COMPANY] about [PAIN], referencing the [TRIGGER_EVENT] and supported by [PROOF].
Hard constraint: zero adjectives anywhere in the email. Nouns, verbs and numbers only. Maximum 90 words. Subject line max 5 words.
Format: SUBJECT / FIRST LINE / PROOF LINE / ASK (one sentence, no question mark — a statement such as "Tell me Tuesday or Thursday and I will send times").
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P004 — Peer Proof First Touch
```text
Write a cold first-touch email where the hero is [PROOF]: a result you delivered for a company like [COMPANY]. Open with the [TRIGGER_EVENT], name the [PAIN] in one sentence, then show the number and how long it took.
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / HOOK LINE / PAIN LINE / PROOF LINE (number + timeframe + source) / ONE-SENTENCE ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 100 words, no superlatives (best, leading, world-class), no exclamation marks.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P005 — Question-First First Touch
```text
You write emails that open with the only question worth asking. Draft a first touch to [ROLE] at [COMPANY] that starts with one specific question about [PAIN], implied by the [TRIGGER_EVENT], and follows with a 25-word answer to why you are asking, plus [PROOF].
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / QUESTION (line one) / WHY I AM ASKING (max 25 words) / PROOF / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 70 words in the body, exactly one question mark, no adjectives.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P006 — Problem-Agitate-Solve
```text
Write a first-touch email using a problem-agitate-solve spine: name the [PAIN] at [COMPANY] (visible through [TRIGGER_EVENT]), show what it costs them in a concrete line, then resolve it with [PROOF] and your [OFFER] in one sentence.
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / PROBLEM / COST / RESOLUTION / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 110 words, no exclamation marks, no more than three sentences before the proof appears.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P007 — Short CEO-to-CEO Note
```text

Format: SUBJECT (max 5 words) / 4 lines total / signature line only.
Hard constraint: maximum 60 words, no bullet points, no adjectives, one question mark at most.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P008 — Referral and Name-Drop Touch

```text
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / NAME-DROP LINE / CONTEXT (max 20 words) / PROOF / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 90 words, the name-drop must be the first sentence, no exclamation marks, and if [MUTUAL] is empty write a version that removes the reference cleanly.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P009 — Observation-First Touch

### P010 — Research-Heavy First Touch

### P011 — Direct Offer First Touch
```text

Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / WHAT IT IS (one sentence) / WHO IT IS FOR / PROOF / PRICE / YES-NO ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 80 words, no exclamation marks, exactly one question mark.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P012 — Loom/Video Teaser Touch
```text
Write a first-touch email that teases a 60-second video you recorded for [COMPANY]: describe in one line what the video shows about [PAIN] after [TRIGGER_EVENT], then give [PROOF] as the reason to click.
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words, no "video" in it) / ONE-LINE TEASE / PROOF / LINK LINE (write "LINK: [VIDEO_URL]") / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 70 words, no adjectives, no exclamation marks, and the video description must be a scene, not a claim.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P013 — Three-Bullet Pain Checklist
```text
Draft a first-touch email to [ROLE] at [COMPANY] that lists exactly three observable symptoms of [PAIN] (each one bullet of max 9 words), ties them to [TRIGGER_EVENT], then offers proof in [PROOF].
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / ONE-LINE OPENER / 3 BULLETS / PROOF LINE / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 100 words total including bullets, no adjectives in the bullets, one question mark maximum, no exclamation marks.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P014 — Pain-to-ROI First Touch
```text
You write emails that do the math. Draft a first-touch email that turns [PAIN] at [COMPANY] (following the [TRIGGER_EVENT]) into a rough monthly cost using only the numbers in [SOURCE], then contrasts it with [PROOF].
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / COST LINE (formula shown as "N x M = $X") / PROOF LINE / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 110 words, no adjectives, no exclamation marks, and any figure not given in [SOURCE] must be labelled "[ASSUMPTION]".
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P015 — First-Line Bank for One Account
```text
Act as a copywriter building a swipe file for one account. Write 20 different first lines for cold emails to [COMPANY], each tied to a different angle on [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN] or [PROOF].
Format: numbered list of 20 lines, each on its own line, each ending with the angle used in brackets, e.g. "01. <line> (TRIGGER)".
Hard constraint: every line max 12 words, no greetings, "I hope this finds you well" is banned, no two lines may share their first three words, total output under 300 words.
Personalization slots required: every line must reference [COMPANY], and the set as a whole must cover [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN] and [PROOF].
```

## Section 2 — Follow-Up Sequences (2nd, 3rd, 4th Touch and Breakup)

### P016 — Second Touch: Value Add
```text
Write the second touch in a sequence to [NAME] at [COMPANY], sent [DAY] after a first email that mentioned [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN] and [PROOF].
Rules: "just checking in" and "circling back" are banned. The second touch must add one new thing: a stat, a question, a link, or a short observation from [SOURCE].
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / NEW VALUE (max 25 words) / ONE-LINE RECAP / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 80 words, no exclamation marks, exactly zero questions in the recap line.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P017 — Third Touch: New Angle
```text
Draft the third touch to [NAME] at [COMPANY] using a completely different angle than the first two, which already used [TRIGGER_EVENT] and [PROOF]. Approach the same [PAIN] from the cost, the risk, or the team impact — pick one and say which.
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / ANGLE LABEL (one word: COST, RISK or TEAM) / BODY (short paragraphs) / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 90 words, no adjectives, no exclamation marks, and the word "follow-up" must not appear.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P018 — Fourth Touch: Proof Stack
```text
Write the fourth touch to [NAME] at [COMPANY] as a proof stack: [PROOF], plus one comparable company result, plus one line from [PROOF_SOURCE]. Tie it to [PAIN] and the reason you first wrote (the [TRIGGER_EVENT]).
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / PROOF 1 (number + timeframe) / PROOF 2 (peer example, max 16 words) / RECAP (one line) / SOFT ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 100 words, no adjectives, no exclamation marks, no question marks — close with a statement such as "Say the word and I will send the two case studies."
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P019 — Breakup Email
```text
You write breakup emails that stay dignified. Draft the final touch to [NAME] at [COMPANY]: state that you are closing the loop, summarise in one line why you wrote (the [TRIGGER_EVENT], the [PAIN], the [PROOF]), and leave one low-pressure door open.
Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words, no "last chance") / 4 lines total / SIGN-OFF.
Hard constraint: maximum 55 words, no guilt-tripping, no exclamation marks, no adjectives, and the word "since" may not open any line.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P020 — Five-Touch Sequence Map
```text
Act as an outreach strategist. Build a 5-touch sequence for [COMPANY] around [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN] and [PROOF], scheduled on Day 0, 2, 5, 9, 14.
Format: markdown table with columns TOUCH | DAY | CHANNEL (email/LinkedIn/phone) | OBJECTIVE (max 6 words) | SUBJECT OR OPENER (max 6 words) | HARD CONSTRAINT (e.g. "max 70 words", "no adjectives").
Hard constraint: no two consecutive touches may use the same objective or the same channel, total output under 450 words, and each touch must carry a distinct constraint.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF] must each appear in at least one row's objective or opener.
```

### P021 — Follow-Up After an Event
```text

Format: SUBJECT (max 6 words) / RECALL LINE (max 14 words, what you both said) / RELEVANCE (max 25 words) / ASK.
Hard constraint: maximum 80 words, no exclamation marks, no "great to meet you", no adjectives about the event.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

```text
Hard constraint: maximum 90 words, no adjectives, no exclamation marks, must be readable by someone who never saw the first email.
```
```text
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```

### P030 — Sequence Tightening Pass
```text
Act as an editor who cuts outreach by half. Here is my current sequence: [SOURCE]. Rewrite all three touches to [COMPANY] so each one earns its send, keeping the [TRIGGER_EVENT], the [PAIN] and [PROOF] but cutting repetition.
Output format: for each touch give BEFORE WORD COUNT / AFTER WORD COUNT / WHAT WAS CUT (max 8 words) / REWRITE.
Hard constraint: each rewritten touch max 90 words, no touch may repeat an idea already made in an earlier touch, no adjectives, no exclamation marks.
Personalization slots required: [COMPANY], [TRIGGER_EVENT], [PAIN], [PROOF].
```
