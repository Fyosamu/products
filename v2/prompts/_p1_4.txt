## Section 7 — Repurposing (Blog to Video, Video to Thread)

### P091 — Blog Post to 60-Second Reel
```text
You convert written work into spoken scripts. Turn this blog post [SOURCE] into a 60-second Reel for [AUDIENCE] on [TOPIC].
Output format:
SOURCE MAP — which paragraph each beat comes from (P1, P3...)
HOOK — taken from the strongest line in the post, rewritten to ≤ 12 spoken words
BEAT 1 — SAY (max 45 words) / SHOT: / SOURCE LINE: "quote from post"
BEAT 2 — same fields
BEAT 3 — same fields
CTA — max 16 words
Constraints: no invented claims, every beat traceable to a paragraph, total spoken 200-260 words, nothing in the Reel that is not in the post.
```

### P092 — Blog Post to 10-Minute YouTube Script
```text
Expand this post [SOURCE] into a 10-minute YouTube script on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].
Output format:
WHAT IS MISSING — 3 things a video needs that the post does not have (research, demo, visual), max 12 words each
COLD OPEN — new, max 60 words
BEAT 1..6 — HEADLINE (from post section) / SPOKEN (max 140 words) / VISUAL: / [SOURCE] PARAGRAPH:
RECAP + CTA — max 50 words
Constraints: no paragraph may be read verbatim on camera, every post section either earns a beat or gets cut (list the cuts), total spoken 850-950 words.
```

### P093 — Video to Eight-Post Thread
```text
Turn this video transcript [SOURCE] into an 8-post text thread for [PLATFORM], on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].
Output format:
POST 1 — the hook, max 40 words, must work without the video
POST 2..7 — one idea each, max 55 words, each ending on a line that pulls to the next
POST 8 — the summary + [CTA], max 45 words
Constraints: exactly 8 posts, no post may repeat an idea from an earlier post, no hashtags, no "thread starts below", total under 380 words, and a final line naming the video timestamp each post came from.
```

### P094 — Transcript to Newsletter Issue
```text
Convert this video transcript [SOURCE] into a newsletter issue for [AUDIENCE] about [TOPIC].

SUBJECT LINE A (max 7 words) / B (max 7 words)

THE LESSON — 3 short sections with H2s, total 300-400 words, drawn from the transcript
ONE-LINE TAKEAWAY — max 20 words
CTA — [CTA], max 20 words
Constraints: no transcript sentence copied verbatim, no more than one link, no exclamation marks in the subject, plainly written (grade 8 or below).
```

### P095 — Podcast Episode to Five Reels


### P096 — Webinar to Short Course Outline
```text
Turn this webinar recording [SOURCE] into a 5-part short course for [AUDIENCE] on [TOPIC].

MODULE 2..5 — same fields
Constraints: 5 modules exactly, no module may exceed the source material, every module ends with a doable action, output under 600 words, and any gap in the source is marked "[NEEDS NEW RECORDING]".
```

### P097 — Long Video to Fifteen Shorts

## Section 8 — Retention & Edit Pattern Prompts

### P106 — Retention Audit of a Script
```text
Act as a retention analyst. Audit this script [SOURCE] for a [LENGTH] video on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].
Output format (table):
TIMESTAMP | LINE (first 6 words) | RISK (drop-off / confusion / repetition / slow) | SEVERITY (H/M/L) | FIX (max 18 words)
Then: TOP 5 FIXES ranked, each with the rewritten line.
Constraints: at least 10 rows, every risk must quote the script, fixes must be rewrites not advice, no row may say "add more energy", total under 600 words.
```

### P107 — Pattern Break Every 20 Seconds
```text
You insert pattern breaks so viewers stop leaving. Take this script [SOURCE] and mark where a break must occur every 20 seconds.
Output format (table):
TIME | CURRENT CONTENT (first 6 words) | BREAK TYPE (question, cutaway, number, POV change, object, silence) | WHAT TO ADD (max 14 words) | LENGTH (sec)
Constraints: no two consecutive breaks of the same type, breaks must add information or reset attention — never filler, at least 8 breaks for a [LENGTH] video, output under 550 words.
```

### P108 — Open-Loop Tracker
```text
Map every open loop in this script [SOURCE].
Output format (table):
LOOP # | OPENED AT (timestamp) | THE QUESTION PLANTED (max 12 words) | CLOSED AT (timestamp) | VERDICT (closed / leaked / never closed)
Then: REWRITE any loop that leaks (gives the answer before the payoff) — max 18 words each.
Constraints: at least 4 loops must be open in a [LENGTH] video, every loop must close before the final 20 seconds or be explicitly stated as a series tease, output under 500 words.
```

### P109 — Cut List
```text
You cut scripts without losing meaning. Here is a draft [SOURCE]. Produce a delete list and a tightened version.
Output format:
DELETE LIST — LINE # | WHY IT GOES (repetition / no stake / throat-clear / meta / slow) — max 8 words each
SAVE LIST — lines that must survive, with reason (max 6 words)
REWRITE — the full script with deletions applied, same order
Constraints: remove at least 25% of words, keep every number and every proper noun, no sentence may gain words, total output shows before/after word counts.
```

### P110 — Cold-Open Restructure
```text
Restructure this script [SOURCE] so the best moment plays at 0:00.
Output format:
STRONGEST MOMENT — identified (timestamp) + why in 10 words
NEW COLD OPEN — max 60 words, spoken, containing no background context
SET-UP MOVED — where the necessary context now sits (timestamp + one line)
CUE LINE — the line that connects 0:20 back to the cold open (max 16 words)
Constraints: nothing may be deleted in this pass, the cold open may not ask a question it answers within 5 seconds, total output under 400 words.
```

### P111 — Pace Map With Delivery Marks
```text
Mark up this script [SOURCE] for delivery: pace, pauses, emphasis.
Output format (table):
LINE | DELIVERY (fast / normal / slow) | PAUSE MARKS (use "/" and "//") | EMPHASISE (bold one word per line) | RUN TIME (sec)
Then: TOTAL ESTIMATED RUN TIME for [LENGTH] and a note if it runs long.
Constraints: exactly one word emphasised per line, no more than 20% of lines marked fast, run time must land within 10% of [LENGTH], output under 600 words.
```

### P112 — Plateau Fix (Saggy Middle)
```text
The middle of this script [SOURCE] sags. Find the plateau and rebuild it.
Output format:
PLATEAU — line range (first 6 words of each end) + diagnosis (max 12 words)
STAKES LADDER — 3 new beats that raise what is at risk: RISK (max 10 words) / EVIDENCE (from [SOURCE] or [NEEDS SOURCE]) / PAYOFF (max 12 words)
REWRITE — the plateau section only, same length as the original or shorter
Constraints: no invented facts, no manufactured drama, the middle must connect to the ending already in the script, output under 500 words.
```

### P113 — Comment Bait Without Frustration
```text
You design comment triggers that do not damage retention. For this video on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE], write 5 comment-bait lines placed at safe moments.

```

### P114 — Mid-Roll Re-Hook
```text
Write a 30-second mid-roll re-entry for this script [SOURCE], placed at [TIMESTAMP].
```

### P115 — Editor's Cleanup Notes

### P116 — Chapter Re-Hook Structure

```text
REWRITE — the full first 30 seconds, each 10-second block carrying at least 2 new facts
Constraints: restatements may not exceed 1 in the rewrite, no background paragraph, rewrite 130 words or fewer, every fact must come from [SOURCE].
```

### P118 — Final 15 Seconds
```text

WHY THIS WORKS — max 12 words
Constraints: no "that's it for this video", no thank-you paragraph, no second CTA after the first, total 48 words or fewer, must land inside 15 seconds at normal pace.
```

### P120 — Retention Scorecard and Rewrite
```text
Score this script [SOURCE] for a [LENGTH] video on [TOPIC].

TOTAL SCORE before and after
Constraints: no axis may score above 8 without quoted evidence from the script, rewrite no longer than the original, no advice-only rows, total under 650 words.
```
