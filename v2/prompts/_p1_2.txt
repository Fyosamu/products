## Section 3 — YouTube Long-Form Scripts & Outlines

### P031 — 10-Minute Educational Full Script
```text
You are a YouTube scriptwriter who writes for the spoken word. Write a complete 10-minute script on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].
Output format:
COLD OPEN (0:00-0:20) — spoken, max 60 words, no intro music description
PROMISE (0:20) — what the viewer has by 10:00, max 25 words
SECTION 1 (0:40) — HOOK LINE / BODY (max 180 words) / PROOF: [PROOF] / TRANSITION
SECTION 2 — same four fields
SECTION 3 — same four fields
SECTION 4 — same four fields
RECAP (9:20) — max 50 words / CTA: [CTA]
Hard cap: 900 spoken words. Mark every b-roll need as [B-ROLL: ...].
```

### P032 — Thesis-Driven Video Essay Outline
```text
You structure video essays. Build an outline for a [LENGTH] essay on [TOPIC] that argues one thesis: [THESIS].
Output format:
THESIS — one sentence, arguable, no hedges
COUNTER-ARGUMENT — one sentence, stated fairly
BEAT 1: OPENING SCENE — the concrete scene that raises the question (max 40 words of description)
BEAT 2..5 — each: CLAIM (max 15 words) / EVIDENCE (source or [PROOF]) / VISUAL (max 10 words) / SO WHAT (max 15 words)
TURN — where the essay admits complexity
CLOSE — returns to beat 1, max 30 words
Constraints: 7 beats max, no beat may rely on a claim not supported by evidence, outline only (no full prose).
```

### P033 — Top-7 Listicle Script
```text
Write a top-7 listicle script about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE], ranking from 7 down to 1.
Output format for each item:
NUMBER — one-line tease (max 12 words) / WHY IT RANKS HERE (max 45 words) / PROOF LINE / VISUAL CUE / 3-SECOND RE-ENTRY LINE (re-hooks viewers who just arrived)
Plus: HOOK (max 16 words), MID-ROLL RE-HOOK after item 4 (max 20 words), CLOSE (max 30 words).
Constraints: items must be distinct (no near-duplicates), total spoken 700-850 words, item 1 must be the one most relevant to [PROOF].
```

### P034 — How-To Tutorial With Chapters
```text
You write tutorials that viewers can follow in real time. Draft a script for a how-to on [GOAL] using [TOOL] for [AUDIENCE], in [LENGTH].
Output format:
HOOK — max 16 words, names the end state
WHAT YOU NEED — 4-6 bullets, max 5 words each
CHAPTER 1 — TITLE (max 6 words) / DO THIS (max 60 words) / SHOW THIS (screen cue) / CHECKPOINT (how the viewer confirms it worked)
CHAPTER 2..4 — same fields
FAILURE NOTE — the most common mistake and its fix (max 40 words)
CLOSE — [CTA] in max 25 words
Constraints: total spoken 700-850 words, every chapter starts with a verb, no step may reference an unlisted tool.
```

### P035 — A vs B Comparison Video
```text
Write a comparison script pitting [OPTION_A] against [OPTION_B] for [AUDIENCE] choosing between them on [CRITERIA].
Output format:
OPENING — frame the decision in one sentence (max 20 words)
ROUND 1: [CRITERION 1] — A CASE (max 45 words) / B CASE (max 45 words) / VERDICT (one line: A, B or TIE + reason max 8 words)
ROUND 2 — same three fields
ROUND 3 — same three fields
WHO SHOULD PICK A — max 30 words
WHO SHOULD PICK B — max 30 words
FINAL CALL — max 25 words including [PROOF]
```


### P043 — Explainer With SAY/SHOW Columns

### P044 — Timestamped Beat Sheet (Outline Only)

### P045 — Deep Dive With a Pattern Break Every 45 Seconds
```text

Output format:
COLD OPEN — max 50 words, moved from the strongest moment anywhere in the draft
Then, per 45-second block:
BLOCK n | TIME | CONTENT (max 25 words) | PATTERN BREAK (re-entry device, max 10 words) | CUMULATIVE PAYOFF (what the viewer knows now that they did not 45 seconds ago)
Constraints: at least 10 blocks, no block may be pure exposition, keep every number from [SOURCE] unchanged, total output under 600 words.
```
